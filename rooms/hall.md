The door closes behind you with less ceremony than you'd hoped.

It is a hall in the way that a corridor with too many doors is a hall. Someone has
been working here — there is a mug, there is a monitor still warm, and there is the
particular silence of a room whose occupant left in the middle of something and fully
intends to come back.

Four doors. None of them are labelled, which you suspect is deliberate rather than
lazy, though you would not swear to it.
