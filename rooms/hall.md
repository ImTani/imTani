# the hall

The door chooses to return back to its original position, slamming shut behind you.

You stand...? You aren't really standing, and there isn't really any ground.
And there is no you, but you are everything. Your consciousness urges you to move.
There are four possible paths, or at least it seems that way.

Oh, and the door doesn't exist.
